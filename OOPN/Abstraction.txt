what is modularity ?

✅ Modularity means dividing a program into independent, smaller parts (modules), where each module handles a specific functionality.

OR
Modularity is breaking a big program into smaller, independent parts (modules), each handling a specific function. This makes the program easy to manage, reusable, and scalable." 🚀
